# ImageGen prompt set

The built-in ImageGen tool edited the exact target-map reference for each plate. The PNGs are the selected outputs. The linked SVGs retain the input geometry.

Preserve its landscape layout, panel numbers and order, body orientation, exact joint/foot coordinates, printed captions and measurements. Limit the edit to line clarity and display polish. Do not invent mechanisms or add visual decoration. Exactly five panels from the source, in order. Fig. 1 Rest, Fig. 2 Lift, Fig. 3 Reach and hold, Fig. 4 Retract raised, Fig. 5 Plant. Keep the dark single three-link leg and small servo/hip block only. Forward is right. Keep the same hinge centers and panel-specific foot marker coordinates. Sample Fig. 2 is vertically up in the exact same fore-aft location; Fig. 3 is horizontally farther right at exactly the same raised height. Fig. 4 returns horizontally while lifted, Fig. 5 returns to the baseline. Match all dimension text exactly: 14 mm up; 17 mm forward and 240 ms. Do not add servo angles. Keep any empty grid cell empty. Every foot marker or joint must remain distinguishable. No additional components, head, logos, watermarks, perspective distortion or freehand measurement text.
