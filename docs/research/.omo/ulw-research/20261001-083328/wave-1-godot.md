# Wave 1: Godot

Official Godot Linux download lists 4.7.2 and MIT license: https://godotengine.org/download/linux/ ; https://godotengine.org/license/ . Linux CameraServer discovers /dev/video devices in pinned source; scene/UI/SubViewport, MultiMesh, shaders provide a strong custom composition foundation. Sources: https://docs.godotengine.org/en/4.7/classes/class_cameraserver.html ; https://docs.godotengine.org/en/stable/tutorials/rendering/viewports.html ; https://docs.godotengine.org/en/stable/tutorials/performance/using_multimesh.html . A concrete GStreamer bridge copies RGB frames to Godot image/texture (not zero-copy): https://github.com/linuxsocke/gd_gst_service/blob/e3354c5a5db656b5cfcee7a09518898b1e7e548d/src/gd_gst_service.cpp#L15-L27 . Kinect v2/depth and tracking remain custom acquisition/perception. OBS window or obs-vkcapture paths untested. `--headless` is no-rendering server mode. No .toe/.tox importer. EXPAND: actual Linux camera/reconnect, renderer capture and RGB/depth visual comparison; all part of deployment gates.
