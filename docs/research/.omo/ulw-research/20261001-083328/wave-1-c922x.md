Official C922-family specs: 1080p30, 720p60, USB 2/UVC 1.1, autofocus and 78-degree diagonal FOV, no night vision: https://support.logi.com/hc/en-001/articles/360023462473-C922-Pro-Stream-Webcam-Technical-Specifications . Linux UVC/V4L2 route is an inference supported by kernel UVC and OBS Linux V4L2 docs, not vendor Ubuntu certification or a test of this unit. USB descriptors and modes on the user's actual camera unmeasured. RGB can feed segmentation, tracking, flow and stylized pseudo-depth; it cannot natively capture IR or measured depth.
