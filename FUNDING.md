## What this funding unlocks

The baskets support non-destructive identification, dimensional measurement, continuity/voltage checks, current-limited bring-up, protocol capture, harness repair, and insulation. They do **not** by themselves make the robot autonomous. The next bill of materials must wait for controller/radio markings, head-button topology, motor voltage and stall current, speaker impedance/label, position-feedback evidence, camera clearances, and radio-site requirements.
